What's changed

This page lists the changes to the Housing Courts Must Change! map that a reader would notice: bills added or removed, new text, data fixes, and corrections when we published something wrong. Newest first. BetaNYC built the map with the Right to Counsel NYC Coalition. It shows which state legislators and coalition members support the campaign's bills.

  • History begins: 2023-11-28, the day the first version of the map went online. Older years are summarized; corrections before that date may be missing.
  • Data refresh: by hand, from time to time. BetaNYC runs a script that refreshes the legislators, their support for each bill, and the coalition's members. Those refreshes are not listed here.

To report a bug in this map or ask for a new feature, open an issue on GitHub.

The Clean Hands Act on the map

February 12, 2026

Tags: Site, Data

The map can now show which legislators support the Clean Hands Act (S4098 and A1621). The Defend Right to Counsel column was removed, the Winter Eviction Moratorium now links to its 2025 bill (S9090 and A10121), and the coalition is described as Right to Counsel NYS.

Every legislator was labeled a Democrat

January 9, 2024

Tags: Site, Correction

From November 28, 2023 to January 9, 2024, the panel that opens when you pick a district said "Democrat" under every legislator's name, including Republicans. The color of the name was right. The panel now shows each legislator's own party.

If you used the old numbers: If you took a legislator's party from that panel before January 9, 2024, check it on the map now or on the State Senate's or Assembly's website.

The map goes online

November 28, 2023

Tags: Site, Data

A statewide map of New York's Senate and Assembly districts that shows which legislators support each bill in the Housing Courts Must Change! campaign, and where the coalition's member and endorsing organizations are, with ways to contact them. It is at hcmustchange.beta.nyc. This was the first version online; the map's credits say it launched in December 2023.
